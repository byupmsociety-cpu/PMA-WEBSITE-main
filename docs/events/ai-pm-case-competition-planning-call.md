## Summary

The PMA AI event moved to this Wednesday, framed as "using AI as a PM": attendees act as PMs analyzing fake user data for a dev copy of the PMA website and shipping features.

**Lessons from last year's vibe coding event**
- Last year's Lovable vibe coding session was weak: remote presenter, hard to follow, poor audio both ways.
- Main challenge: attendees span a wide range of AI experience, from beginners to Claude Code users.

**Event format ideas**
- Daniel proposed a ship something by the end of the night framework, with prompts on the PMA site that attendees' AI can follow.
  - Options: online portfolio, personal project, solve a problem
  - Paying members could link portfolios; some featured on the site
- Nathan suggested AI across the product lifecycle, timed mini team competitions, or skill based learning tracks.
- Heber proposed an MCP with a fake company database as a PM case study for advanced users.
- Converged on fake user data for a dev version of the PMA website; attendees fork it, analyze, ship features.
  - Best ideas could be incorporated into the real site
  - Entry level attendees likely need a small side group

**Luma registration and branding**
- Luma questions to gauge skill: select all AI tools used (Lovable, Claude), project experience scales, link to projects.
- Messaging should be PM focused ("using AI as a PM"), not a generic AI upskilling event or hackathon.
- Plan may be simplified if registrants skew entry level; backup is a slide deck on AI in the PM cycle plus stations.

**Timing and marketing**
- Event was planned for the 19th, but Nathan will be away; moved up to this Wednesday.
- Daniel and Heber both have Wednesday open; this is their ownership event.
- Last Friday's company visit drew about 20 people; their VP of product discussed Claude workflows.
